My name is Lucas "Patator" Marti, and it's been a while since I've wanted to challenge myself to share publicly a little bit of what I do. Admittedly it's not often much and not often better than what has been done elsewhere. But I want to get out of my comfort zone and share a little more of my creative output to the world. 

Here you can expect a little bit of coding, a little bit of reviews of stuff I have appreciated. And occasionally other stuff but don't expect anything grand. This blog and anything susceptible to feed it will very rarely be the priority above my family or my job.
